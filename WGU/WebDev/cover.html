Although I didn't publish everything I created, the experience was one the I enjoyed and learned a lot from. I used a number of different platforms as I was learning to code, but ultimately the language the watches utilized was C and the operating system, while proprietary, was a Real-Time Operating System (RTOS). Thus, though my experiences with the watch I wore years ago, I have experiences with many of the technologies utilized in this job, as outlined by the job description. I have more recent experience in these platforms as well, this example was meant as a demonstration that the themes sought after have been the focus, to varying extents, of my software development and engineering career from the beginning.

I believe I would be a good fit with this company for a number of reasons. To begin and iterate past, I have been a fan of this company for a number of years. While my personal use of my account may be hit and miss, I have interacted with these technologies in a number of professional contexts, using and helping others to use a variety of other accounts through the years. This process of learning to execute has been a theme throughout much of my professional career, and is something that, in my own way, I have learned to enjoy.
